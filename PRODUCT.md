# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public readers exploring free, published image-based research about Taiwan stocks and industries. They use the Library to identify relevant research and review it before investment decisions. Signed-in readers can save favorites and write reviews; author and reader access remain separate.

## Product Purpose

PocketStocks organizes Taiwan-industry research into browsable books and an image-based reader. It helps readers follow company, supply-chain, and technology signals, understand the research context, and open the material relevant to their next decision. Library-wide disclosure makes clear that AI-generated research may contain errors and that important information should be checked against sources.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, editorial. The experience should feel considered and trustworthy while keeping the research content easy to scan.

## Anti-references

- Repetitive bordered boxes, nested cards, and decorative rules that compete with book covers and reading hierarchy.
- Generic dashboard or marketplace styling that makes research feel like inventory rather than editorial material.
- Copying YouTube branding, video controls, viewing metrics, or channel and subscription concepts; only its search-first, thumbnail-led discovery rhythm is a reference.
- AI-generated artwork or claims that imply verified provenance or conclusions the product does not have.

## Design Principles

1. Help readers orient quickly to the subject and relevance of each research book.
2. Put the AI-generation and possible-error disclosure where readers encounter the Library context.
3. Make research easy to scan, open, and revisit before a reader acts on it.
4. Keep private reader features and author administration distinct from public browsing.
5. Use visual emphasis to support understanding rather than to decorate the interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast requirements. Preserve semantic headings and native controls, keyboard operation, visible focus, and interactive targets of at least 44px where specified by the product guidance. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep the AI-generation notice readable as normal supporting text and available in semantic reading order.
